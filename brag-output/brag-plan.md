# Brag Plan: Fintr

## What is this app?
Fintr is an AI personal finance assistant that turns receipts and manual entries into categorized transactions, budgets, and a clear month — so you can save more and spend smarter.

## The angle
A receipt becomes the month. Cafe Luna lands as dining, the numbers settle, and the tagline is the product: save more, spend smarter, afford the life you want.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Cream canvas, navy type, the real headline: "Save more. Spend smarter."

## Key moments (the middle)
- A receipt card resolves one field at a time: Cafe Luna, ₱248, Dining, then Logged.
- A dark month view lands three numbers — in, out, net — then a dining budget bar stops inside its limit.

## Outro / punchline
The name, then the rest of the headline: "Afford the life you want." Then "On the App Store."

## User flow worth showing
Snap or enter a receipt → Fintr extracts merchant, amount, and category → the month's in / out / net and a category budget update. Amounts in the video are fictional stand-ins, not customer data.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet premium product film
- Interpretation: four scenes, slow holds, mixed-case League Spartan, soft crossfades, no joke, no feature grid.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20.5

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #FAF9F7 marketing cream; dashboard scenes #151921
- Accent: #0D9488 signal teal, used as a bar and a logged state, not as small body text
- Text: #0A2540 on cream; #F4F1EA on dark cards (#1E2433)
- Display font: League Spartan
- Body font: League Spartan
- Strongest visual element: the landing headline and the dark dashboard number row

## Share copy (draft)
Fintr turns a receipt into the month. Save more. Spend smarter.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-9-by-ende-dot-app.mp3
- Music treatment: fade in over 0.45s, hold low under the picture, fade out across the last 1.9s
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset `skills/brag/assets/music/cues/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-9-by-ende-dot-app.music-cues.md`. Strong cues used: 6.34s merchant, 10.54s Logged, 12.65s first month number. Beat grid for the other receipt fields and the remaining stats: 7.40, 8.44, 13.70, 14.76.
- Audio-reactive treatment: none — skipped so the render stays seek-safe without an extraction helper
- SFX posture: sparse, low high-frequency-risk clicks on each extracted field, one soft impact when the month numbers arrive
- Audio-coupled moments: receipt fields, Logged pill, in/out/net cards
- Restraint rule: no whooshes, no voiceover, no beat-locked body copy
